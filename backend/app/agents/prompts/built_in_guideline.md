# Built-in guideline: AI-generated image defects

Always active on every project, alongside any brand guideline. Rule ids are cited
in every defect comment.

## ANATOMY — human and animal form

- `ANAT-01` **Hands and fingers.** Wrong finger count, fused or forked fingers, extra
  knuckles, fingers bending against the joint, thumbs on the wrong side, hands melting
  into objects they hold.
- `ANAT-02` **Limbs and joints.** Extra or missing limbs, arms attached at the wrong point,
  elbows or knees bending backwards, limbs of mismatched length or thickness, a limb that
  disappears behind a body and does not re-emerge correctly.
- `ANAT-03` **Faces.** Asymmetric eyes, mismatched pupils or gaze directions, teeth of
  irregular count or size, ears at different heights, a second row of teeth, melted or
  smeared facial features.
- `ANAT-04` **Skin and hair.** Hair strands that merge into skin or clothing, waxy plastic
  skin texture, skin tone shifting across one body, hairlines that dissolve.
- `ANAT-05` **Repeated or blended people.** Two people sharing a limb, a background figure
  duplicated, a person merging into another person or into furniture.

## PHYSICS — how the world works

- `PHYS-01` **Gravity and support.** Objects floating without support, resting at impossible
  angles, or balanced on nothing.
- `PHYS-02` **Contact and grip.** Hands not actually touching the object they hold, objects
  passing through hands, fingers wrapping around nothing, a grip incompatible with the
  object's shape.
- `PHYS-03` **Intersections.** Objects clipping through each other, straps or handles that
  pass through solid material, jewellery merging into skin, limbs through furniture.
- `PHYS-04` **Light and shadow.** Shadows falling in inconsistent directions, missing shadows
  under grounded objects, reflections that do not match the scene, multiple contradictory
  light sources.
- `PHYS-05` **Scale.** Objects sized wrongly relative to their surroundings, a product much
  larger or smaller than it could be in a human hand.
- `PHYS-06` **Wrong action.** A person performing an action incoherently — holding a cup that
  pours the wrong way, using a tool backwards, sitting on nothing, walking with both feet
  planted identically.

## ARTIFACT — generator failure modes

- `ARTF-01` **Text.** Garbled or nonsensical lettering, misspelled words, letterforms that
  dissolve, inconsistent typefaces within one sign or label.
- `ARTF-02` **Pattern breakdown.** Repeating patterns that warp, tile incorrectly, or lose
  structure — fabric weaves, tiles, brickwork, foliage.
- `ARTF-03` **Structural incoherence.** Architecture that cannot exist, stairs to nowhere,
  windows misaligned with floors, furniture with impossible joinery.
- `ARTF-04` **Edge and texture failure.** Melted or smeared boundaries between objects, an
  object's outline dissolving into the background, unnatural blur inconsistent with depth
  of field.
- `ARTF-05` **Duplication.** Objects unintentionally repeated, background elements cloned,
  symmetry that is too perfect to be real.
- `ARTF-06` **Product integrity.** For commerce imagery: the product itself is deformed, its
  logo warped, its proportions altered, or its details invented. Treat any defect on the
  product being sold as more severe than the same defect in the background.

## Severity guidance

- `blocker` — a viewer would notice it, and publishing it would embarrass the brand.
  Anything wrong with the product being sold, or with a person's hands or face in frame.
- `warning` — noticeable on inspection, defensible either way, worth a human decision.
- `nitpick` — only visible when zoomed in; polish rather than a real problem.

## What is not a defect

Do not report: intentional stylisation, artistic lighting, shallow depth of field, motion
blur, deliberate abstraction, grain or film effects, legitimate cropping, or a subject
simply being unusual. Style is not a defect. Only report what is *wrong*.
